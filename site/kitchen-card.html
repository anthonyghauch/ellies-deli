Running a night

  1. Opening: switch Taking orders on (top of the page).Turn it off when the kitchen closes. Customers then see “Kitchen's closed”.
  2. New orders show up on their own, oldest first, one line each.The page refreshes every 15 seconds and new orders flash green.
  3. Made it? Tick Ready.The customer gets an email and their phone shows “Your order is ready!”.
  4. They paid? Tick Paid (card or cash).The bar at the top shows what's collected and what's still owed.
  5. Ran out of something? Open the Menu tab and switch it to Sold out.Customers see it greyed out right away. The Hungry Man deal on/off switch is at the top of that tab.
  6. Cancelled or a test order? Tap the trash icon on its row to delete it.Customers can cancel on their own until you tick Ready. Use Unpaid, To make or search to find orders.
